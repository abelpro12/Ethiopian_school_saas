Welcome to Seattle Academy

Seattle School has been a beacon of educational excellence in Addis Ababa for over 25 years. Founded with a vision to provide world-class education to Ethiopian students, we have consistently delivered outstanding academic results and shaped the future of thousands of young minds.

Our commitment to holistic education ensures that every student develops not just academically, but also morally, socially, and emotionally. We believe in nurturing well-rounded individuals who are prepared to contribute positively to society.
