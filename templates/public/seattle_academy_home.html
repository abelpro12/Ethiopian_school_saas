Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents Ask

What grade levels does Seattle Academy cover?

Seattle Academy provides comprehensive education starting from Kindergarten (KG 1, KG 2, KG 3) up through Primary (Grades 1-6), Middle School (Grades 7-8), and High School / Preparatory (Grades 9-12).

How can parents track their child's grades and attendance?

Parents are provided credentials to log into our dedicated Seattle Academy SaaS Portal. From the portal, parents can view real-time attendance, grade mark sheets, conduct reports, fee invoices, and communicate directly with teachers.

Are bus transportation and cafeteria services available?

Yes, Seattle Academy provides bus transportation covering key sub-cities across Addis Ababa, alongside campus dining facilities with nutritious meal options.
