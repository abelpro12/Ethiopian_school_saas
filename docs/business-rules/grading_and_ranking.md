# Grading and Ranking Business Rules

1. **Grade 9-12 Continuous Assessment**: Standard weighting is 10% Assignments, 10% Quizzes, 20% Midterm, 60% Final Exam (100% Total).
2. **Competition Ranking (1224 Rule)**: Ties consume rank positions. If two students tie for Rank 1 with identical scores, both are assigned Rank 1, and the next student receives Rank 3.
