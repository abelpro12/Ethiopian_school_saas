# Business Rules: Continuous Assessment & Ethiopian Grading System

## Continuous Assessment Weighting (Sum = 100%)

- **Assignment:** 10%
- **Quiz:** 10%
- **Midterm Exam:** 20%
- **Final Exam:** 60%
- **Total:** 100%

## Mark Entry & Approval Workflow

```text
Draft → Submitted (Teacher) → Approved (School Admin) → Published → Locked
```
